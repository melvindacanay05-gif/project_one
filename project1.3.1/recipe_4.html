Chicken Pesto Panini Sandwich

at a glance

  • Prep time: 10 minutes
  • Cook time: 8–10 minutes
  • Yield: 1 sandwich
  • Difficulty: Easy

ingredients

  • 1 panini roll or ciabatta roll
  • 1 small cooked chicken breast, sliced
  • 1–2 tablespoons basil pesto
  • 2 slices mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tomato slices
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil or softened butter

instructions

  1. Prepare the bread. Slice the roll in half and spread pesto on the inside of each half.
  2. Add the filling. Layer the sliced chicken, mozzarella, and tomato on the bottom half.
  3. Close the sandwich. Place the top half of the bread over the filling and lightly brush the outside with oil or butter.
  4. Toast the panini. Place in a preheated panini press or skillet. Cook until the bread is crisp and the cheese has melted, turning once if using a skillet.
  5. Serve. Let cool briefly, slice in half, and serve warm.
