Egg, Ham & Cheese Croissant

at a glance

  • Prep time: 5 minutes
  • Cook time: 10 minutes
  • Yield: 1 sandwich
  • Difficulty: Easy

ingredients

  • 1 large croissant
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 slices deli ham
  • 1 slice cheddar or Swiss cheese
  • 1 teaspoon butter
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

instructions

  1. Prepare the croissant. Slice the croissant horizontally and set aside.
  2. Cook the egg. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the egg until the white is set and it reaches your preferred doneness.
  3. Warm the ham. Heat the ham in the skillet for about 30–60 seconds per side.
  4. Add the cheese. Place the cheese over the warm ham and allow it to soften.
  5. Assemble. Place the ham, cheese, and cooked egg inside the croissant.
  6. Serve. Serve warm.
