Southwest Chicken Wrap

at a glance

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Cook time: 10–12 minutes
  • Yield: 1 large wrap
  • Difficulty: Easy

ingredients

  • 1 large flour tortilla
  • 1 grilled chicken breast, sliced
  • ¼ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tomato slices, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons sliced red onion
  • ¼ cup black beans, drained and rinsed
  • ¼ cup yellow corn
  • ½ cup mixed greens
  • ¼ fresh avocado, sliced
  • 2 tablespoons ranch dressing

instructions

  1. Cook the chicken. Season and grill the chicken breast until fully cooked, reaching 165°F (74°C) internally. Rest briefly, then slice.
  2. Prepare the ingredients. Drain and rinse the black beans, prepare the corn, slice the tomato and onion, and slice the avocado.
  3. Layer the wrap. Place the mixed greens on the tortilla. Add chicken, cheddar, tomato, red onion, black beans, corn, and avocado.
  4. Add dressing. Drizzle ranch dressing over the filling.
  5. Fold the wrap. Fold in the sides, roll tightly, and slice in half.
  6. Serve. Serve immediately while the chicken is warm.
