Blueberry Muffin

at a glance

  • Prep time: 15 minutes
  • Cook time: 20–25 minutes
  • Yield: 12 muffins
  • Difficulty: Easy

ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • ¾ cup milk
  • ½ cup melted butter, cooled
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups fresh or frozen blueberries

instructions

  1. Preheat the oven. Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Line a 12-cup muffin pan with paper liners or grease it lightly.
  2. Mix the dry ingredients. Whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
  3. Mix the wet ingredients. In another bowl, whisk the egg, milk, melted butter, and vanilla.
  4. Make the batter. Stir the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients just until combined. Gently fold in the blueberries; avoid overmixing.
  5. Fill the pan. Divide the batter evenly among the muffin cups.
  6. Bake. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until golden and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  7. Cool and serve. Let the muffins cool in the pan for a few minutes, then transfer to a rack.
