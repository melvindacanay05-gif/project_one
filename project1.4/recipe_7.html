Chamomile Tea

at a glance

  • Prep time: 2 minutes
  • Cook time: 5 minutes
  • Yield: 1 cup
  • Difficulty: Easy

ingredients

  • 1 chamomile tea bag or 1 tablespoon dried chamomile flowers in an infuser
  • 1 cup freshly boiled water
  • Honey or lemon, optional

instructions

  1. Heat the water. Bring fresh water to a boil, then let it rest briefly so it is no longer at a rolling boil.
  2. Steep the tea. Place the tea bag or infuser in a mug. Pour in the hot water and steep for 5 minutes, or follow the package directions.
  3. Remove the tea. Remove the tea bag or infuser. Avoid squeezing the bag if you prefer a gentler flavor.
  4. Finish and serve. Add honey or lemon if desired and serve warm.
