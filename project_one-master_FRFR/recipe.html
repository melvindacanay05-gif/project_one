at a glance

  • Prep time: 18 hours
  • Cook time: 30 minutes
  • Yield: 2 loaves
  • Difficulty: Easy

Choose a theme

ingredients

  • 512 g all-purpose flour or bread flour
  • 10–15 g kosher salt
  • 8 g instant yeast
  • 455 g lukewarm water
  • Butter or oil for greasing the pan
  • 4 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • Flaky sea salt, to taste
  • 1–2 tsp fresh rosemary leaves (optional)

instructions

  1. Mix the dough. Whisk together the flour, kosher salt, and instant yeast in a large bowl. Add the lukewarm water and mix with a spatula until a sticky dough forms.
  2. Rest and fold. Cover the dough and let it rest for 30 minutes. With a wet hand, stretch one edge of the dough upward and fold it toward the center. Repeat 8–10 times around the bowl.
  3. Refrigerate overnight. Lightly coat the dough with olive oil. Cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 12 hours, preferably 18–24 hours, or up to 3 days.
  4. Prepare the pan. Grease two 8–9 inch round pans or one 9 × 13 inch pan with butter or oil. Use two forks to release and gently deflate the dough. Divide it between the round pans, or leave it whole for the rectangular pan.
  5. Let the dough rise. Coat the dough with oil and let it rise at room temperature for 3–4 hours, until puffy and airy.
  6. Dimple the dough. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Drizzle the remaining olive oil over the dough. Press your fingertips deeply into the surface to create dimples. Add rosemary, if desired, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
  7. Bake. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the focaccia is golden brown and crisp underneath.
  8. Cool and serve. Transfer the bread to a cooling rack. Let it cool for at least 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
